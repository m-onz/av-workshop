## You can damage your ears!

Please do not experiment with pure data with headphones on - you can damage your ears.

Always use speakers or your headphones away from your ears - until you are supremely confident it's safe.

## Be careful with visuals

If you have photo sensitive epilepsy be careful creating strobe effects. 

If you look directly into a projector you can blind yourself.
