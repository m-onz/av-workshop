## You can damage your ears!

Please do not experiment with pure data with headphones on - you can damage your ears.

Always use speakers or your headphones away from your ears - until you are supremely confident it's safe.

...

## Be careful with visuals

If you have photo sensitive epilepsy be careful creating strobe effects. 

If you look directly into a projector you can blind yourself.


## The workshop

If you have any trouble, don't worry as computers have internal state, and even a single integer (whole number) can be infinite.

That means there is an infinity of ways software can go wrong and one of the skills you build up as a software programmer / developer is the knack.

The knack is an elusive intuition that you build up over time, getting upset, sad or stressed scares away this energy.

Like a zen master appreciate the exquisite mathematical improbability of anything you build actually working at all and relax.
